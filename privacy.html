Hearth — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Hearth is a companion app for the tabletop game Dungeons & Dragons. This policy explains, plainly, what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: Hearth collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere.

What we collect

Nothing. Hearth does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-up, no logins, and no analytics or tracking of any kind.

Where your data lives

Everything you create in Hearth — your characters, notes, settings, and any photos you add — is stored only on your own device, in the app's local storage. It never leaves your device and is never sent to us or to any third party. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, that information is gone, because it only ever existed on your device.

Internet use

Hearth is designed to work fully offline and does not require an internet connection. Two optional features can use the internet only if you choose to use them:

  • Text recognition (OCR): if you use the optional feature to read text from an image, the app loads a recognition library over the internet at that moment. The image is processed on your device; it is not uploaded to us.
  • Optional AI features: if you choose to connect your own AI service by entering its address, requests go directly to the service you specify. Hearth does not provide, operate, or receive any of that data.

If you never use those features, Hearth makes no network connections at all.

Children's privacy

Because Hearth collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new "last updated" date.

Contact
